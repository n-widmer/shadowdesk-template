# How to ask for things

## In one line

Brief me the way you would brief a sharp new hire. What you want, what good looks like, and what you know that I do not.

## The picture

A new hire on day one. Very capable, no ego, and knows nothing about your business. They have never met your client, never seen your pricing, never heard the history. Where the picture breaks down: a new hire picks up your business by hanging around for a month. I do not. I only know what you tell me, or what is written in your folder.

## Why you care

Most bad answers trace back to a thin brief. Show me an email you liked instead of describing the tone. Tell me what is in your head: the client went quiet in March, the quote is due Friday. Ask for the outcome, "get this quote out today," rather than the steps. Then stop me mid job and redirect me. Nothing is wasted.

## What this looks like in your setup

`/shadowdesk:email` writes better when you paste a real email you liked first. `/shadowdesk:skill-builder` asks questions for the same reason. `CLAUDE.md` tells me to stop and explain when you sound lost. So this works: "I do not understand what you said, say it again like I am not technical." Use it often.

## Try this

Find an email you sent and liked. Paste it in chat and say: "Write one like this to [name], about [the thing]." You should get back a draft that sounds like you, not a template.
