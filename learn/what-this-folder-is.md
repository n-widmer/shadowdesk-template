# What this folder is

## In one line

This folder on your computer is the thing you bought. Claude Code is the program that lets me read and write inside it, and the chat panel is where you talk to me.

## The picture

Think of your own workbench in a workshop. Every tool you set on it stays there. Come back next week and it is all still where you left it. A normal chat with an AI is closer to renting a tool for an hour. The job gets done, you hand the tool back, and the shop keeps nothing of yours. One limit: a bench does not fill itself. It gets better only if you keep adding to it.

## Why you care

Your work stacks up here. Your notes on a customer from March, how you price a job, how you word the follow up to a client who went quiet. It stays in files you own, so I can read it before you ask. You stop explaining your business from scratch every time.

## What this looks like in your setup

Open the folder and you see plain files. `CLAUDE.md` holds how I work with you. `SKILLS.md` lists what I can already do. `clients/` holds one folder per customer, `projects/` one per job. What each folder is for: `references/folder-layout.md`. What the installed programs are: `references/whats-getting-installed.md`.

## Try this

In chat, ask me to write a short note in your projects folder about the one job you want off your plate this week, then read it back to you. You should see me name the file I made and say your own words back. That file is on your computer now.
