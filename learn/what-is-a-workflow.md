# What a workflow is

## In one line

A workflow is several steps strung together so one ask finishes a whole job, instead of you steering each step.

## The picture

Think of your best employee. Ask them to write a quote and you get a good quote. That is a skill. One job, done well.

Now think of the process written on the wall. A job comes in, gets quoted, gets sent, gets scheduled, gets invoiced. Several hands, and every handoff was decided before anyone started. That is a workflow.

The limit worth naming: paper waits for a person to pick it up. A workflow here runs the steps on its own once you ask.

## Why you care

A skill is a thing you ask for. A workflow is a thing that runs. The line is blurry, and a big skill can hold a workflow inside it. They are not rival products.

So you stop asking for one step at a time, and you decide up front what happens when a step comes back empty.

## What this looks like in your setup

Take a new lead. Today `/shadowdesk:email` drafts your reply in your voice, from the voice profile `/day-one` saved in `onboarding/`. I can log the lead in a folder under `clients/`. Looking them up and setting a follow-up need a connected tool; `CONNECTIONS.md` shows what you have hooked up. `/skill-builder` turns those steps into one run.

## Try this

In chat, say: "Walk me through what happens when a new lead emails me, and mark which steps you can do today." You should see a numbered list, each step marked ready or not yet.
