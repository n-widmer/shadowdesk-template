# What a skill is

## In one line

A skill is one job, written down in plain words, so I do it the same good way every time instead of a slightly different way each time.

## The picture

A recipe card. The card is not the meal. It is the steps for the meal, written down by the person who cooks it best, so it comes out right whoever is at the stove. A skill is the card. The work I hand you is the meal. Where the card falls short: a recipe never asks a question. A skill can stop and ask you something before it goes on.

## Why you care

You stop re-explaining. Once "how I chase an unpaid invoice" is written down, you never say it again, and the March one reads like the January one. You can read the card yourself too, so when a draft comes out wrong, you fix the words.

## What this looks like in your setup

Nick wrote the ones that came with your folder. `/shadowdesk:email` drafts an email in your voice. `/shadowdesk:teach` explains something to you. `SKILLS.md` is your own catalog of them. They are instructions, not code: the email one is a file at `.shadowdesk/plugins/shadowdesk/skills/email/SKILL.md`, and you can open it and read every step. To make a new one, `/skill-builder` turns something you already do by hand each week into a skill. For ideas, read `references/automation-menu.md`.

## Try this

In chat, type: "What skills do I have? Pick one and read me the steps in it." You should see a list, then plain sentences. No code.
