# How Claude remembers you

## In one line

Each chat has a memory that fills up, and it goes away when that chat ends. What survives is whatever got written into a file in your folder.

## The picture

Our chat is a whiteboard. We fill it fast, and when the session ends it gets wiped clean. Your folder is the file cabinet in the corner of the room. Anything I copy off the whiteboard and file away is still there tomorrow. Anything I do not copy is gone. The one place the picture breaks down: a cabinet does not fill itself, so the copying has to happen before the wipe.

## Why you care

Closing out a session is not busywork. Do it and next week I still have the client, the quote, and the follow up you were chasing. Skip it and you explain the whole job again from scratch. Starting a fresh chat is normal and healthy. It is not a loss, as long as the last one was closed out.

## What this looks like in your setup

When you are done working, type `/shadowdesk:end-session`. It writes the session into your notes, updates `SKILLS.md` and `CONNECTIONS.md`, and saves a copy online. Customer work lands in `clients/`. Everything else lands in `projects/`. I also keep short notes about you outside the folder, so you do not re-explain your business every time. `references/memory.md` covers what those notes hold and what they leave out.

If I seem to have forgotten something, say so, and tell me where it was written down. I will go read it.

## Try this

In your chat, type: show me my memory. You should see the short notes I keep about you, sorted into groups. Correct any that are wrong.
