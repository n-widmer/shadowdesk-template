# What a skill is

## In one line

A skill is one job written down in plain words. It tells me how you want that job done, so I do it the same way every time.

## The picture

A recipe card. The card is not the meal. It is the steps for the meal, written by whoever cooks it best, so it comes out right at any stove. A skill is the card. The work I hand you is the meal. Where the card falls short: a recipe never asks a question. A skill can stop and ask you something before it goes on.

## Why you care

You stop re-explaining. Once "how I chase an unpaid invoice" is written down, you never say it again, and the March one reads like the January one. You can read the card yourself too, so when a draft comes out wrong, you fix the words.

## What this looks like in your setup

Nick wrote the ones that came with your folder. `/shadowdesk:email` drafts an email in your voice. `/shadowdesk:teach` explains something to you. `SKILLS.md` is your own catalog of them. They are instructions, not code: the email one is a file at `.shadowdesk/plugins/shadowdesk/skills/email/SKILL.md`, and you can open it and read every step. To make a new one, `/shadowdesk:skill-builder` turns something you already do by hand each week into a skill. For ideas, read `references/automation-menu.md`.

## Try this

In chat, type: "What skills do I have? Pick one and read me the steps in it." You should see a list, then plain sentences. No code.
