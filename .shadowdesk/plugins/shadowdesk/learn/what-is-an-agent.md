# What an agent is

## In one line

An agent is an AI that takes action and picks its own next step toward a goal, not one that only answers.

## The picture

Picture two people at your front desk. One answers what you ask, then waits. You hand the other a job: sort out the Miller quote. She pulls the file, checks last year's price, writes the quote, and comes back when it is done. The second one is an agent. Where she stops: she asks before she sends it or drops the price, and you want her to.

## Why you care

You hand over a whole job, not one step at a time. Chase the client who went quiet. Get the Miller quote out. Your part moves from doing the work to saying yes or no to what comes back. More jobs: `references/automation-menu.md`.

## What this looks like in your setup

Everything you have read about works as one. Skills are what I know how to do, listed in `SKILLS.md`. Workflows are how I run those skills in order. Connected tools are how I reach past your laptop, tracked in `CONNECTIONS.md`. `CLAUDE.md` holds the rules I work by. On a big job I hand a piece to a helper agent so our chat stays clear. You may see that. It is normal.

## Try this

In chat, type: "Read SKILLS.md and CONNECTIONS.md, then tell me one thing you could do for my business today." You should see me open both files first, then name one job. Getting what I need before I answer is the agent part.
