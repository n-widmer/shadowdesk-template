# What this folder is

## In one line

This folder on your computer is your setup. It is the thing itself, not a copy of it. Claude Code is the program that lets me read and write inside it. The chat panel is where you talk to me.

## The picture

Think of your own workbench in a workshop. Every tool you set on it stays there. Come back next week and it is still where you left it. A normal chat with an AI is closer to renting a tool for an hour. The job gets done, you hand it back, nothing stays. One limit: a bench does not fill itself.

## Why you care

Your work stacks up here. Notes on a customer from March, how you price a job, how you chase a client who went quiet. It stays in files you own, so you stop explaining your business from scratch.

## What this looks like in your setup

Open the folder and you see plain files. `CLAUDE.md` holds how I work with you. `SKILLS.md` lists what I can already do. `clients/` holds one folder per customer. `projects/` holds work that is not about one customer. What each folder is for: `references/folder-layout.md`. What got installed on your laptop: `references/whats-getting-installed.md`.

## Try this

In chat, name the one job you want off your plate this week. Ask me to write it into a short note in your projects folder and read it back. You should see me name the file I made and say your words back. That file is on your computer now.
