# Start here

This folder is the plain-English basics of how your setup actually works. It is here for the moments when something stops making sense.

Jump to whatever is confusing you right now. Read that one page, then go back to work. The order below is a suggestion, not a rule. If you are starting cold, start with the first one.

The eight pages:

[what-this-folder-is](what-this-folder-is.md), what the folder on your laptop is and why your work lives in it.

[what-is-a-skill](what-is-a-skill.md), the saved instructions behind a command like `/shadowdesk:skill-builder`.

[what-is-a-workflow](what-is-a-workflow.md), several steps strung together into one job you ask for once.

[what-is-an-api](what-is-an-api.md), how one tool lets another one in, and what a key is for.

[what-is-an-mcp-server](what-is-an-mcp-server.md), what makes an outside tool like Gmail reachable from your chat.

[what-is-an-agent](what-is-an-agent.md), what is happening when I go off and work on something on my own.

[how-claude-remembers](how-claude-remembers.md), what I keep between sessions and what I forget.

[how-to-ask-for-things](how-to-ask-for-things.md), how to word a request so you get back what you wanted.

If you would rather not read any of it, ask me to walk you through one out loud. Say something like "explain skills to me, use my business as the example." I will talk it through in plain words, using your own clients and your own work as the example. Stop me with questions whenever you want.
