# How Claude remembers you

## In one line

Each chat has a memory that fills up, and it is gone when the chat ends. What survives is only what got written into a file first.

## The picture

Our chat is a whiteboard. We fill it fast, and when the session ends it gets wiped clean. Your folder is the file cabinet in the corner. Anything I copy off the whiteboard and file away is still there tomorrow. Anything I do not copy is gone. The one place this picture breaks down: a cabinet does not fill itself, so the copying has to happen before the wipe.

## Why you care

Closing out is not busywork. Do it and next week I have the client, the quote, the follow-up you were chasing. Skip it and you start over. A fresh chat is normal, not a loss, if you closed out.

## What this looks like in your setup

When you are done working, type `/shadowdesk:end-session`. It writes the session into your files, updates `SKILLS.md` and `CONNECTIONS.md`, and saves a copy online if your backup is on. Customer work lands in `clients/`, other work in `projects/`. I also keep short notes about you outside the folder, so you do not re-explain your business. `references/memory.md` covers what those notes hold and leave out.

If I seem to have forgotten something, say so and point me at where it was written.

## Try this

In your chat, type: show me my memory. You should see the short notes I keep about you, sorted into groups. Correct any that are wrong.
