# What a workflow is

## In one line

A workflow is several steps strung together so one ask finishes a whole job, instead of you steering each step.

## The picture

Think of your best employee. Ask them to write a quote and you get a good quote. That is a skill. One job, done well.

Now picture the process written on the wall. A job comes in, gets quoted, gets scheduled, gets invoiced. Several hands, every handoff decided before anyone started. That is a workflow.

Paper has one limit: it waits for a person to pick it up. A workflow here runs the steps on its own once you ask.

## Why you care

You ask once and go do something else. The lead gets logged, the reply gets drafted, the follow up gets set, without you steering each turn.

You also settle up front what a step should do when it comes back empty, instead of me guessing.

## What this looks like in your setup

Take a new lead. `/shadowdesk:email` drafts your reply. Once you have run `/capture-voice`, it writes in your voice, from the profile that lands in `onboarding/`. I can log the lead under `clients/`. Looking them up and setting a follow up need a connected tool. `CONNECTIONS.md` lists what you have hooked up. `/shadowdesk:skill-builder` strings those steps into one run.

## Try this

In chat, say: "Walk me through what happens when a new lead emails me, and mark which steps you can do today." You should see a numbered list, each step marked ready or not yet.
